Privacy Policy

1. Controller

Malner Studios
Email: malnerstudios@proton.me

2. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how personal data may be processed when you use the FoxCount mobile app. FoxCount is a mental arithmetic training app.

3. Data processed by FoxCount

FoxCount does not require a user account and does not ask users to submit registration details such as name, postal address or phone number. Training progress, scores, settings and similar app data may be stored locally on the user’s device in order to provide app functionality and display progress. This locally stored data is not transmitted to Malner Studios by FoxCount.

If you contact us by email, we process the information you provide, such as your email address and the content of your message, solely to handle your request.

4. Advertising through Google AdMob

FoxCount uses Google AdMob to display ads in the app. For this purpose, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process information such as device information, IP address, advertising identifiers, app interactions, ad interactions, diagnostics, crash logs and performance data.

Google and its advertising partners may use this information to deliver ads, limit ad frequency, measure ad performance, prevent fraud and abuse and, depending on user consent and settings, provide personalized or non-personalized advertising.

5. Consent management

Where legally required, FoxCount may display a consent message when the app is opened. This message may allow users to choose whether certain advertising-related data processing is permitted. Where available in the app, users can revisit and change their privacy choices later.

6. GDPR legal bases

  • Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for processing necessary to provide requested app functions.
  • Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for legitimate interests such as support, maintenance and protection of the app.
  • Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR where processing is based on consent, especially for consent-based advertising purposes.

7. Retention

Local app data remains on the device until it is deleted by the user, the app data is reset or the app is uninstalled. Support emails are retained only as long as necessary to handle the request and comply with applicable legal obligations. Data processed by Google is subject to Google’s own retention periods and policies.

8. Recipients and international transfers

Data may be processed by Google and its advertising partners, including recipients outside the user’s country of residence, such as in the United States. Where required by law, international transfers are based on appropriate safeguards.

9. Privacy rights

Where applicable, users may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection. Where processing is based on consent, consent can be withdrawn at any time with future effect. Users may also lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority.

10. U.S. state privacy notices

Depending on applicable law, users may have rights to know, access, correct or delete personal information and to opt out of certain data sharing or targeted advertising. FoxCount does not sell personal information for money.

11. Children’s privacy

FoxCount is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information directly, please contact us.

12. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is published on this page.

Datenschutzerklärung

1. Verantwortlicher

Malner Studios
E-Mail: malnerstudios@proton.me

2. Geltungsbereich

Diese Datenschutzerklärung erläutert, wie personenbezogene Daten bei der Nutzung der mobilen App FoxCount verarbeitet werden können. FoxCount ist eine App zum Training von Kopfrechenfähigkeiten.

3. Durch FoxCount verarbeitete Daten

FoxCount erfordert kein Nutzerkonto und fragt keine Registrierungsdaten wie Name, Postanschrift oder Telefonnummer ab. Trainingsfortschritt, Punktestände, Einstellungen und ähnliche App-Daten können lokal auf dem Gerät gespeichert werden, um die App-Funktionen bereitzustellen und den Fortschritt anzuzeigen. Diese lokal gespeicherten Daten werden durch FoxCount nicht an Malner Studios übermittelt.

Wenn du uns per E-Mail kontaktierst, verarbeiten wir die von dir übermittelten Informationen, insbesondere deine E-Mail-Adresse und den Inhalt deiner Nachricht, ausschließlich zur Bearbeitung deiner Anfrage.

4. Werbung über Google AdMob

FoxCount verwendet Google AdMob zur Anzeige von Werbung in der App. Zu diesem Zweck kann das Google Mobile Ads SDK Informationen wie Geräteinformationen, IP-Adresse, Werbekennungen, App-Interaktionen, Werbeinteraktionen, Diagnosedaten, Absturzprotokolle und Leistungsdaten verarbeiten.

Google und seine Werbepartner können diese Informationen verwenden, um Werbung auszuliefern, die Häufigkeit von Werbung zu begrenzen, die Leistung von Werbung zu messen, Betrug und Missbrauch zu verhindern und – abhängig von der Einwilligung und den Einstellungen der Nutzer – personalisierte oder nicht-personalisierte Werbung bereitzustellen.

Soweit anwendbar, bestehen Rechte auf Auskunft, Berichtigung, Löschung, Einschränkung der Verarbeitung, Datenübertragbarkeit und Widerspruch. Je nach anwendbarem Recht können Rechte auf Auskunft, Zugang, Berichtigung oder Löschung personenbezogener Informationen sowie Widerspruch gegen bestimmte Datenweitergaben oder zielgerichtete Werbung bestehen. FoxCount verkauft personenbezogene Informationen nicht gegen Geld.
